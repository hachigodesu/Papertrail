The first release of Papertrail, a library manager and reader for academic publications, for Windows and Linux.

Papertrail indexes your papers, textbooks, theses, reports and standards where they already are, and never changes them. It stays offline until you choose otherwise.

## Downloads

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `Papertrail-1.0.0-setup.exe` | Windows 10 and 11. Installs just for you, or for everyone |
| `Papertrail-1.0.0-portable.zip` | Windows 10 and 11, from any folder or a USB stick, keeping its library beside it |
| `Papertrail-1.0.0-x86_64.AppImage` | Most Linux distros, from Ubuntu 22.04 and Debian 12 on |
| `Papertrail-1.0.0.flatpak` | Any Linux distro with Flatpak. Installs the KDE runtime from Flathub |
| `SHA256SUMS` | Checking a download is the one published |

The Windows downloads aren't code-signed, so Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What it does

- A library of tens of thousands of publications, with tabs, filters, tags, collections and a search box that understands fields
- A reader for PDF, EPUB, DjVu, MOBI, AZW3, XPS, CBZ and FB2, with highlights and notes that never change your files
- Searching inside documents, and inside your highlights and notes
- Details from Crossref, OpenAlex, arXiv and Open Library, reviewable and undoable, and warnings of retractions
- Journal issues split into their articles
- References in Harvard, APA, IEEE and Chicago styles, and BibTeX, RIS and CSL-JSON export
- Moving a library to another computer, even with its files in different folders
- Summaries from Claude with your own API key, straight away or at half price
- Daily backups

See the [README](https://github.com/hachigodesu/Papertrail#readme) for how to install it and what it sends online.
