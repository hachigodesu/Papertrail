Papertrail 1.0.2, a library manager and reader for academic publications, for Windows and Linux.

## What's new

- **Saved searches.** Keep a search with its tab and filters in the sidebar, and show it again with a click. Right-click **Saved searches** to save the current one. They come across when you export your library.
- **A Settings window** under **File > Settings...** (Ctrl+,). It holds a light or dark theme for the window, separate from the reader's page colours, what documents open with (page layout, page colours and text size), and the options that were in the File menu.
- **Articles in an issue.** Right-click an issue and choose **Show its articles** to list them in the library, in the order they're in it. Search for them yourself with `in:"issue title"`.
- **Retractions on covers.** A retracted, partly retracted or withdrawn publication has a red bar saying so along the bottom of its preview.
- **Pictures on dark pages** keep their own colours, rather than looking like negatives. Scanned pages still go dark.

## What's changed

- An issue's contents leave out entries that are only numbers, such as "1", "2", "3", when its articles have titles.
- The list of an issue's articles in the details wraps long titles, with the pages beneath each.
- Highlighting is in the menu from right-clicking selected text, so the Highlight button has gone from the reader's toolbar.

Opening your library in 1.0.2 adds saved searches to it, after backing it up. Once it has, 1.0.1 can't open it.
